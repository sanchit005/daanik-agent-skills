# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our commitment
We aim to make Daanik Everyday AI a welcoming, respectful and practical community. Contributors should be treated with dignity regardless of experience, background, identity, language, ability or level of technical knowledge.

## Expected behaviour
- Be respectful, constructive and specific.
- Assume good intent while discussing the effect of words and actions.
- Welcome questions and explain unfamiliar concepts without ridicule.
- Accept feedback and focus on improving the work.
- Protect privacy and do not share another person's personal information.
- Disagree with ideas without attacking people.

## Unacceptable behaviour
Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, doxxing, sexualised abuse, deliberate disruption and repeated personal attacks are not acceptable.

## Maintainer response
Project maintainers may edit, reject or remove contributions and may restrict participation for conduct that violates this policy. Reports should be made privately through GitHub's repository contact or reporting facilities.

This code applies to project spaces and public interactions where someone is representing the project.

## 1. What this skill does
This skill reduces the mental load of everyday household and personal administration. It turns vague jobs into checklists, shopping lists, schedules and practical options. It can help with chores, errands, meal planning, routine paperwork and shared responsibilities.

It does not assume a particular family structure, gender role, culture, diet, income or living arrangement.

## 2. When to use it
- "Help me organise a weekly grocery list."
- "I have guests this weekend. Help me plan the preparation."
- "Make a household cleaning checklist."
- "What documents should I gather before applying for this service?"
- "Help us divide household tasks fairly."

Do not use it as a substitute for emergency services, medical advice, legal advice or official confirmation of government requirements.

## 3. How the person can start
**Starter prompt:**  
"Help me organise [task]. I have [time/budget/people available]. My constraints are [list]. Please make a simple checklist and tell me what to do first."

A person can share incomplete information. Ask only what is needed to avoid an unsafe or unusable plan.

## 4. Step-by-step workflow
1. **Clarify the outcome.** Identify the task, due date, available time, budget and constraints such as allergies, accessibility, location or number of people.
2. **Break it down.** Create actions in a sensible order. Identify dependencies and tasks that can happen in parallel.
3. **Prioritise.** Separate essential, helpful and optional work. Avoid turning a simple task into an elaborate system.
4. **Make lists usable.** Group shopping items by category or store section, and distinguish things already available from things to buy when information is supplied.
5. **Plan meals flexibly.** Ask about dietary restrictions and preferences when relevant. Never infer them from a person's name, location or background.
6. **Coordinate fairly.** Assign owners only when people have agreed or the user provides the allocation. Do not assume chores belong to a particular gender or family member.
7. **Verify official steps.** For forms, bills, public services and registrations, prepare a checklist but direct the user to the issuing authority for current rules, dates and fees.
8. **Include a fallback.** Offer a simpler version if time, energy or budget is limited.
9. **Review.** Produce a concise checklist and highlight what remains uncertain.

## 5. Recommended response format
- **Outcome and deadline**
- **Do first**
- **Checklist**
- **Shopping or document list** (if relevant)
- **Who is responsible** (only if provided)
- **Low-effort alternative**
- **Items to verify**

## 6. Examples
**User:** "Create a grocery list for five dinners."

Ask about allergies or dietary restrictions and whether the user wants vegetarian, non-vegetarian or mixed meals. Suggest dishes, consolidate ingredients and avoid assuming pantry stock.

**User:** "Help me prepare for a family event in two days."

Create a timeline by day and deadline, separating essential arrangements from optional decorations. Flag dependencies such as confirmed guest count or venue.

## 7. Behavioural design
Reduce cognitive load through chunking, batching and checklists. Use choice architecture to simplify options without hiding alternatives. Do not shame people for an untidy home, missed chores or limited resources. Keep routines flexible and consensual.

## 8. Privacy, safety and limits
- Do not request identity documents or private family records unless strictly necessary; generally provide a checklist instead.
- Ask about allergies when food suggestions could matter; do not make medical or food-safety assurances.
- Do not submit forms, make payments, book services or contact relatives unless the user authorises the action and a connected tool confirms it.
- Protect the privacy of children and other household members.
- Verify current official requirements with the relevant authority.

## 9. Before responding, check
Are constraints respected? Are assumptions visible? Is the list practical and inclusive? Is there a lower-effort fallback? Are official details clearly marked for verification?
